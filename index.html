FAQ

Fair questions. Straight answers.

Is this just another budgeting app?
No. Budgeting apps track what you spent. Operator analyzes your complete financial picture and tells you what to do next — the decisions, the order, and the reasoning.
Is this financial advice?
Operator provides personalized financial analysis and education, not regulated investment advice. We don't sell investment products or manage money. When your situation calls for a licensed professional — a CPA, attorney, or fiduciary advisor — we connect you with a vetted one.
How is this different from a financial advisor?
Most advisors are built for people who already have assets to manage, and are compensated accordingly. Operator is built for the building phase: no asset minimums, no products to sell you, and a flat, transparent price.
Is my financial data safe?
Safer than most, because of what we don't collect. We never ask for logins, passwords, account numbers, or your SSN — so nothing we hold could be used to access or move your money. What you do send travels over an encrypted connection, is never sold or shared with advertisers, and is deleted whenever you ask.
Is the instant review written by a person?
No, and we say so on the report itself. The $29 review is computed from the numbers you enter — which is exactly why it is instant, cheap, and cannot invent a figure about you. Every number in it traces back to something you typed. What it cannot do is exercise judgment, ask you a follow-up question, or weigh two goals against each other. That is what the human review tier is, and it is a person reading your full picture — not a machine draft with a signature on it.
Can the instant review get something wrong?
It can only be as right as what you enter — it does arithmetic on your answers, so a wrong input gives a wrong output. What it will never do is make something up, because it has no ability to. If your situation has complications the form didn't capture, the review will be accurate and still incomplete, and that is precisely the gap human review fills.
What do I need to have ready?
Nothing to upload — you type the numbers in. Helpful to have nearby: roughly what you take home, your usual monthly costs, and a list of what you owe with balances and interest rates. Round numbers are fine; we need the shape of your finances, not the decimals. About ten minutes.
How long does it take to get my report?
The instant review appears on screen the moment you finish the intake — there is nothing to wait for and no email to check. If you add human review, that comes back within five business days.
What if my finances are a mess?
That's who this is for. Messy is not a disqualifier — it's the starting point. The bigger the tangle, the more valuable the map.
What if I don't make much money?
Operator works at any income. The roadmap is built around your actual numbers, not a hypothetical high earner's.
Do you guarantee results?
No, and you should be suspicious of anyone who does. We guarantee a rigorous, human-reviewed analysis and a clear plan. Executing it is yours.
Will you tell me to stop buying coffee?
No. We don't do lectures. If a spending pattern is materially blocking your goals, we'll quantify it and show you. Small pleasures are not your problem.
Can Operator help me decide whether to buy a house?
Yes — it's one of the most common questions we answer. You'll get a readiness assessment based on your actual numbers, plus the specific path to "ready" if you're not there yet.
Can it help me decide whether to start a business?
Yes. We'll show you what your finances can support, what runway you'd need, and what to shore up first — so the decision is grounded in numbers, not nerves.
Is this credit repair?
No. We don't dispute items on your credit report. We address the underlying structure — debt, cash flow, habits — that determines your financial health, credit included.
Do you sell investments or insurance?
Never. We have no products, no commissions, and no incentive to steer you anywhere. You pay for analysis, and analysis is all we sell.
What happens after the 90 days?
Many clients run a follow-up Reset to re-map their position and set the next 90 days. Others take the roadmap and run. Both are wins.
Do I have to talk to anyone on the phone?
No — and you can't, even if you wanted to. Everything is online, start to finish: you fill out the intake, your report arrives by email, and any questions you have after are answered in writing. Nothing to schedule, no meeting to sit through.
What if I disagree with a recommendation?
Every recommendation comes with its reasoning, so you can pressure-test it. You can also submit questions about your report and get a reviewed response.
Why wouldn't I just ask a free AI chatbot?
You can — and it will give you a confident, generic answer without ever verifying your numbers. Operator's analysis is built from your actual documents and reviewed by a person who is accountable for what ships.
