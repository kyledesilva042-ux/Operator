Operator provides personalized financial analysis and education, not regulated investment advice. We don't sell investment products or manage money. When your situation calls for a licensed professional, we connect you with a vetted one.

Yes. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is strictly limited, and we never sell your data. You can request full deletion at any time.

A trained financial analyst reviews every report before delivery — checking the analysis and signing off on every recommendation.

No, and you should be suspicious of anyone who does. We guarantee a rigorous, human-reviewed analysis and a clear plan. Executing it is yours.
