1. What we are, and what we aren't

Operator is not a registered investment advisor, broker-dealer, law firm, accounting firm, credit repair organization, or lender. We do not manage money, hold assets, execute trades, sell financial products, or earn commissions from anyone.

What we sell is analysis, in two forms. The instant review is software: it performs arithmetic on the figures you enter and returns a structured assessment. No person reads it before you do, and we say so on the review itself. The human review is a person reading your complete picture and writing an assessment with judgment applied. Both are educational and organizational work. Neither is a recommendation to buy or sell any specific security, and neither is tax or legal advice.

Because the instant review is generated automatically, it is only as accurate as what you type into the intake, it cannot ask you a follow-up question, and it cannot know anything you did not tell it. It will never fabricate a figure — every number traces to one of your inputs — but it can be accurate and still incomplete. Treat it as a well-organized starting point, not a verdict.

You make every decision. You are responsible for what you do with the report, including verifying anything material with a licensed professional. We will tell you plainly, in the report itself, when something should go to a CPA, an attorney, or a licensed advisor — and we do not take referral fees when we do.

We do not guarantee results. Nobody honestly can. Past outcomes for other clients do not predict yours, and any number in a report is a projection built on the information you provided.

2. Terms of service

2.1 The agreement

By buying a service or submitting an intake form, you agree to these terms. If you're purchasing for someone else, you confirm you have their permission to share their financial information.

2.2 What you're buying

One of two things, depending on what you bought:

  • The instant review is a software-generated analysis, displayed in your browser immediately after you complete the intake. It is delivered the moment it appears on your screen. You can save it as a PDF; we recommend you do, since it lives in your browser rather than in an account.
  • Human review and planning is a written analysis prepared by a person, delivered by email, at the scope described on the pricing page.
  • Delivery timing for human review is measured from when we receive your completed intake, not from your payment. If your intake is incomplete or the numbers don't reconcile, we'll email you, and the clock pauses until you reply.
  • Questions about your report are included by email for 30 days after delivery. That means clarifying what we wrote — not a second analysis of a changed situation.
  • Revisions are included where we got something wrong or missed something you told us. A materially changed situation (new job, new debt, new goal) is a new engagement.

2.3 What you owe us

Accurate information. The report is only as good as the intake. If you understate a debt or omit an obligation, the plan will be wrong, and that isn't a defect we can refund.

2.4 Your report is yours

You own your report and can do whatever you like with it privately. You may not resell it, publish it as your own work, or use it as a template for a competing service. We keep the rights to our frameworks, formats, and methods.

2.5 Cancelling a recurring plan

We may decline or refund an engagement — before or after payment — if it needs licensed advice we can't give, if the situation involves active litigation or bankruptcy proceedings, or if we can't do useful work with the information available. You get your money back in full when we do this.

3. Refund policy

Before we've delivered: full refund, no questions, any time. Just ask.

The instant review is delivered the instant you finish the intake, so there is no "before delivery" window for it. We refund it anyway, within 14 days, if it wasn't useful to you — just ask, and you don't have to justify it. We would rather refund you than have you feel taken.

Human review: full refund any time before we deliver. After delivery, tell us what missed within the same window and we'll fix it or refund it. "I already knew some of this" isn't a defect, and neither is a plan that's hard to execute.

Refunds return to the original payment method and typically post within 5–10 business days.

4. Privacy policy

4.1 What we deliberately never collect

This is the important part, so it goes first. We do not ask for and will never request:

  • Bank, brokerage, or credit card logins or passwords
  • Full account numbers
  • Your Social Security number
  • Anything else that could be used to access or move your money

If you ever receive a message appearing to be from us asking for any of the above, it isn't from us. Forward it to .

4.2 What we do collect

  • What you send us: your name, email, and the figures and context you enter in the intake form.
  • Payment information: handled entirely by our payment processor. We see the last four digits and the amount; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic site analytics: if analytics is enabled, aggregate page views with no cookies and no cross-site tracking. We cannot identify you from it.

4.3 What we do with it

We use your information to produce your report, deliver it, answer your questions about it, and keep the records a business is required to keep. That's the list. We do not sell your data, rent it, share it with advertisers, or hand it to anyone selling financial products. Nobody gets a lead list with your name on it.

4.4 Who else touches it

Only the vendors it takes to run the service — a payment processor, an email provider, a form host, and hosting. Each sees only what it needs, and each is bound by its own terms. We do not transfer your information to anyone else without your explicit say-so, unless the law requires it.

4.5 Automated analysis

The instant review is generated automatically by software running in your own browser. Your figures are used to compute it on your device; the review itself is not assembled on our servers. Human review, when you buy it, is a person reading your submitted intake. We do not permit your financial information to be used to train third-party AI models, and we do not make automated decisions that produce legal effects for you — the output is analysis you choose what to do with.

4.6 How long we keep it

Your intake is retained while your engagement is active and for two years after, so we can answer questions and meet record-keeping obligations. After that it's deleted.

Separately, your answers are saved in your own browser's local storage so you can close the intake and come back to it, and so your review can be regenerated on that device. That copy never leaves your device and is not visible to us. Clearing your browser data removes it — which is why we suggest saving your review as a PDF.

4.7 Your rights

Email us and we will, within 30 days: send you a copy of everything we hold about you, correct anything wrong, or delete it entirely. No fee, no justification needed. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under laws like the CCPA or GDPR; we extend the rights above to everyone regardless.

4.8 Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in access-controlled systems. No system is perfect, which is exactly why we designed the intake so that a breach of our records still could not be used to access your accounts.

4.9 Children

This service is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
